An antilibrary is the shelf of books you own and have not read. The term is
Eco's by way of Taleb: a private library is not a trophy case of what you know
but an instrument for keeping the size of your ignorance in view, and the
unread part is the part doing that work.

I keep this page for the same reason. Everything here is something I have
decided is worth not having read yet: a known gap rather than an absence I
have never noticed. The list gets longer faster than the [library](/philosophy/library)
does, which is the honest ratio.
