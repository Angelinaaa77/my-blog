something I had been treating as obvious turned out to be a choice. That is a
higher bar than "enjoyed", and a much higher bar than "agreed with". Two of
the books here I think are substantially wrong, and I would not give them up.

The covers link out. Where I have a note, it is about what the book did to me
rather than what it says.
