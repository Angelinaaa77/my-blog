I have never found a satisfying way to introduce myself at a party, which is
probably why I keep a page like this one. The short answer is that I am
someone who reads more than is strictly useful and writes in order to find out
what I think. The longer answer is below, and it changes.

## Who am I?

Mostly a set of habits with a name attached. I notice that I am drawn to
problems where the difficulty is definitional rather than technical, where
the hard part is working out what would even count as an answer. That
disposition has shaped my work, my reading, and the kind of arguments I enjoy.

The parts I would defend: I would rather be usefully wrong than vaguely right.
I think most disagreements that look substantive are two people using one word
for two things. I am suspicious of my own fluency, because being able to
explain something quickly is not the same as understanding it.

## Where do I come from?

Somewhere specific, which I will fill in. But the honest version of this
question is not geographic. It is about the inherited assumptions you only
notice when someone raises an eyebrow: the ones that felt like the structure
of reality until you met a reasonable person who had never considered them.

I came from a place and a set of people who took ideas seriously without
taking themselves seriously, and I have been trying to hold onto both halves
of that ever since.

## Where am I going?

What matters most in my life. Game. Romance. The future.

Characteristics that I continuously remind myself to carry on

- Preternatural confidence with humility
- Unusual determination with diversification of risk

Ideal lifestyle: coffee, game, and money at all hours; playing poker into the night.

My principle of making money is simple. The only way to accumulate wealth is through value creation. I want mine built on my own niche ability: assets that earn while I sleep, which is Naval Ravikant's definition of wealth.

I always need new challenges and bigger canvas.

> "The hell with it. I am just going to run it myself,"
> Simons told a friend.
